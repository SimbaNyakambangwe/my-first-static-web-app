Zimbabwe entry requirements

Please check Zimbabwe's entry requirements well before you travel. Passport and visa requirements depend on your nationality and circumstances; also check any transit requirements for countries you will pass through.

If you need a visa, check your eligibility and application requirements through Zimbabwe's official government eVisa portal. Allow time for processing before departure and confirm the latest guidance with the immigration authorities and your airline.

Official Zimbabwe eVisa application portal ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Apply through the official portal, not our travel-plans form. Please do not send us passport or payment details.
